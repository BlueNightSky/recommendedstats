    Stat targets

    Current vs. target for Haste, Crit, Mastery and Versatility, with a progress bar and a clear verdict: too low, on target, or over.

    The most-worn item per slot, with enchant and gem picks, a runner-up, and a NEW tag when the top pick changes.

    The full tree with the build top players agree on, for every boss and every dungeon. One click copies the loadout string.

    Separate targets, gear and talents for each, never mixed. Raid data falls back Mythic, Heroic, Normal only when it has to.
