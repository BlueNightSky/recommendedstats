# Questions and fixes

*Answers to the things people ask most.*

**The panel shows my stats but no verdict.**

You're in combat or in an instance, where Blizzard restricts reading exact stat values. The verdict comes back once you're out.

**My spec says there's no data.**

There weren't enough top players of that spec for that content yet. The panel says so rather than showing wrong numbers. It fills in as data is refreshed.

**A boss shows "(overall build)".**

That boss had too few kills with builds to read from, often early in a tier, so your spec's overall build is shown. It changes once more kills come in.

**How often is the data updated?**

Stats, BiS and talents are refreshed with releases as new content and patches land. The priorities are refreshed about once a month or after a patch, and the window shows the date they were read.

**Will the priorities raise my DPS?**

Not on their own. They show how top players play your spec and what matters most, to learn from. They don't react to the fight you're in.

**Can I translate it?**

Yes. Copy `RecommendedStats/Locale/enUS.lua`, translate the text on the right of each line (keep every `%s` and `%d`), and send it on Discord or GitHub.
