# Game versions

*What Recommended Stats shows on each version of the game.*

One download works on every version: it loads the right data for your client by itself.

| Version | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Retail** | Everything in this wiki, Raid and Mythic+ |
| **MoP Classic** (beta) | Haste, Crit and Mastery targets, Hit and Expertise caps, BiS, talents and glyphs |
| **TBC Anniversary** (beta) | Crit, Hit and Haste targets, BiS with the ranged slot, talent splits |
| **Classic Era and Season of Discovery** (beta) | Hit and Crit from gear, BiS, talent splits |
| **WoW Forever** | Coming soon |

The Classic versions are raid only and don't have the Priorities window yet.
