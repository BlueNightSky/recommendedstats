# Priorities

*What top players press, in order, with their opener and cooldown timing.*

Click the **Priorities** tab, or type `/rs priorities`. It follows your current spec and hero talents.

1. Leave the dropdown on **Overall** for all bosses together, pick one raid boss, or pick **Mythic+: bosses** or **Mythic+: trash pulls (AoE)**.
2. Read the three columns left to right.
3. Hover any icon for the spell's tooltip and a line on how top players use it.

### The three columns

- **Opener:** the presses most top players make at the pull, in their usual order.
- **Priority:** one numbered list. First, what top players press the moment it's ready, quickest first. Then spenders and other core presses, by what one press is worth. Filler last. A spell that spends a proc says so on its row, like "When Hot Streak! is up".
- **When to use CDs:** when each long cooldown is first used, how often, and what it's pressed with.

![The priorities window for one raid boss](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/rs-rotation.webp)

Healers get the Priority column only, since healing cooldowns follow the boss's damage, not a fixed timing.

![Priorities for a healer](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/rs-rotation-healer.webp)

> It's a guide to how your class works and what matters most, not a "press this now" helper. It reads nothing about the fight you're in.
