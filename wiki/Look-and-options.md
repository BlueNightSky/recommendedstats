# Look and options

*Row sizes, skins, moving the panels and the options page.*

### Row sizes

Four sizes, from one compact line to a full readout: **Default**, **Small**, **Medium** and **Large**. Pick one from the dropdown next to the Raid/Mythic+ toggle, or in the options.

### Skins

**Default**, **Class Color** or **Custom Color**. Share a look with `/rs skin export` and load someone else's with `/rs skin import`.

### Moving the panels

The panels attach to the character screen by default. Detach them in the options to drag them anywhere. Positions are remembered per character; `/rs resetpos` puts them back.

### Options

Esc > Options > AddOns > Recommended Stats, `/rs options`, or right-click the minimap button:

- Window position: attached or free.
- Show or hide the Stats tab and the BiS button.
- Show the minimap icon.
- Colorblind-friendly icons.
- Row size and skin.
