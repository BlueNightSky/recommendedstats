# Getting started

*Install it, open your character screen, and pick Raid or Mythic+.*

Recommended Stats shows your secondary stats against what top players of your spec run, plus their best-in-slot gear, talent builds and a priority list of what they press, right beside your character screen.

### Install

1. **CurseForge app:** search for **Recommended Stats** and click Install.
2. **By hand:** download it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/recommended-stats) and put the **RecommendedStats** folder in `World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns`.
3. Start the game, or type `/reload`.

### First use

1. Press **C** to open your character screen. The Recommended Stats panel appears beside it.
2. Pick **Raid** or **Mythic+** at the top, so the targets match what you're doing.
3. Use the tabs along the top: **Stats**, **BiS**, **Talents** and **Priorities**.

Everything follows your current spec, and hero talents where it matters.
