# Your pulls vs top players

*Compare your own logged raid pulls with the top players of your spec.*

Click **My pulls** at the top of the priorities window, or type `/rs link`. Press **Ctrl+C** to copy the link and paste it into your browser.

It opens your page on the [RecommendedStats Analyzer](https://rs.ctrlshiftzed.com):

1. Your raid nights from Warcraft Logs, with every kill and wipe.
2. Pick a pull to see what to work on first.
3. Then your presses per minute and cooldowns, next to the top players of your spec on that boss.
