# Best-in-slot gear

*The best item per slot, with the enchants and gems top players use.*

Click the **BiS** tab, or type `/rs bis`.

- The best item for every slot, for your class, spec and content (Raid or Mythic+).
- The **enchant and gem** top players use on it.
- **How many of the top players** wear each item.
- Status marks that take your own item level into account.
- Hover an item for its tooltip.
